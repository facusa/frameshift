OUR LITTLE STORY

Born from one very picky dog.

Milo turned his nose up at almost everything. So our founder, June, started hunting for better snacks, simpler ingredients, and toys that could survive more than one afternoon.

Friends started asking where she found them. Then neighbors did. A tiny shelf became a tiny shop, and Milo & Maple was born.

“If we wouldn’t bring it home to Milo or Maple, it doesn’t make the shelf.”
